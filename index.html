Welcome to Suryodaya Adventures

Ilam's most exciting adventure escape, minutes from Kanyam Tea Garden

Tucked into the rolling hills between Kanyam, Fikkal and Shree Antu, Suryodaya Adventures was built for travellers who want their Ilam tourism itinerary to include something more than photos of tea rows — real, heart-racing adventure activities in Ilam, framed by the same Tea garden views that made this region famous.

Whether you're a family exploring things to do in Kanyam, a couple chasing the Shree Antu sunrise, or a group of friends after a full adrenaline day, our park is designed to be Ilam's benchmark for outdoor adventure, capped off with a proper local meal.

After the Adrenaline

Refuel with home-style Ilam cuisine

Once the harnesses come off, settle in at our on-site restaurant for a hearty Nepali thali, fresh momos, and locally grown Kanyam tea. Our kitchen sources vegetables and dairy from nearby farms, so every plate tastes like Ilam.

  • Traditional Nepali thali & seasonal local dishes
  • Fresh Kanyam tea grown minutes from the property
  • Outdoor seating with valley & hillside views
  • Vegetarian, non-vegetarian & group meal options
