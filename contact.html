Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How far is Suryodaya Adventures from Ilam Bazaar?

We're located on the Kanyam–Fikkal road, roughly a 45–60 minute drive from Ilam Bazaar depending on traffic and season.

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking ahead is recommended, especially for weekends, holidays and groups of 6 or more, to guarantee activity slots.

Is the park suitable for young children?

Yes — sky cycling, archery and camping are family-friendly, while activities like quick jump have minimum age requirements.
