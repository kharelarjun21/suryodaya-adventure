Adventure Activities in Ilam

Six ways to get your adrenaline going near Kanyam

Every activity is briefed and supervised by trained local guides, with graded difficulty so first-timers and thrill-seekers both leave happy.

Ziplining Sky Cycling Quick Jump Wall Climbing Archery Camping
Most Popular

Ziplining

Clip in and launch across the valley on our signature zipline, with sweeping views over the tea-covered hills of Kanyam. It's the activity most first-time visitors ask for by name — and the one they come back to repeat.

Age 8+ Beginner friendly Full safety harness Guide supervised

Family Favourite

Sky Cycling

Pedal a single-rail cycle suspended high above the ground, balanced by a secure overhead safety line. It looks daring in photos but feels controlled and steady — a favourite with families and couples looking for a shared thrill.

Age 6+ Great for kids Overhead safety line
Book This Activity
Adrenaline

Quick Jump

A controlled free-fall drop from a raised platform, harnessed and braked for a short, sharp burst of adrenaline. It's over in seconds — but it's the activity guests talk about longest.

Age 12+ Weight & age restrictions apply Staff braked descent
Book This Activity
Skill Builder

Wall Climbing

Multiple graded routes let beginners build confidence on gentler holds while experienced climbers push for the top. Every climb is belayed by a trained guide from the ground.

Age 8+ Graded routes Belay supervised
Book This Activity
Precision

Archery

Take aim at our target range with coaching on stance, draw and release. It's a calmer counterpoint to the zipline and quick jump — popular with couples, groups and anyone who wants to slow down and focus.

Age 10+ Beginner coaching included Group scoring rounds
Book This Activity
Overnight

Camping Under the Stars

Extend your day into an overnight stay on the hillside. Pitch a tent, gather around the bonfire for local snacks and stories, then rise early to catch the sunrise light over the surrounding ridges before breakfast.

All ages welcome Bonfire included Tent & gear provided
